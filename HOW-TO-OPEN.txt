Concorde v0.14.0

To open it: unzip this folder, then double-click index.html. It opens in your browser. Nothing to install.

LIVE MODE (default when you open the file from your computer): real sign-in, chats, photos, invites and Tell the Tower, through your Supabase project.
  1. Enter your email. You get a code by email. Enter it. (First time only: choose your name; the very first person needs no invite code.)
  2. To try chat you need a second person: invite a friend from Settings > Invite a friend (needs email sending set up, see backend/SETUP.md),
     or sign in on a second browser (or a private window) with another email you own.
  3. Calls: open a chat and press Start call (groups) or the phone / video buttons (one-to-one). Your browser asks to use the
     microphone the first time: choose Allow. To test with one computer, use a second window (private window, different email).
     Needs the LiveKit pass function and the updated schema, see backend/SETUP.md.

DEMO MODE (made-up friends and a pretend call, nothing sent anywhere): open index.html?mock  (add ?mock to the address).
  In a browser address bar that looks like  file:///.../index.html?mock

Version number and date are in the bottom-right corner. They live in js/version.js.
